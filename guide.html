How it works

Mangia Mangia

Our shared recipe box. Anyone with the link can add, cook, rate, and argue. Here's the whole thing on one page.

Getting in

  • Open the link. It asks Who's cooking? — tap your name, or type it if you're new. No password; it remembers you on that phone or computer.
  • On someone else's device, tap the name at the top-right to switch to yours.
  • On a phone, use Share → Add to Home Screen and it behaves like an app, works with no signal, and gets the little castle icon.
  • The ☾ button switches light and dark. Either way the tablecloth stays Italian.
  • The QR button next to your name is the invite: show it to a friend's phone camera, send the link, or Print for the fridge.

Finding something to cook

  • Search any word: an ingredient, a tag, part of a name, "Mom".
  • Course chips (Starters, Pasta & Soups, Mains…) and Cuisine chips filter the list. Tap All to clear.
  • Cooks row: tap a face to see one person's recipes. Family recipes shows the heirlooms, Award winners the ones with a trophy, From the web the imports.
  • Sort by Newest, Top rated, A–Z, or Quickest. ★ Favorites are yours alone.
  • What should I make? (the die on a phone) picks one at random. Set a time limit first if you're hungry now.
  • The fridge button at the end of the search box: type what you've got, and it shows what you're closest to making and what's missing. Salt, oil, butter and the other basics are assumed unless you untick the box.

Adding a recipe

  • + Add a recipe to type one in. Ingredients go one per line with the amount first (2 cups flour, 1/2 tsp salt). Start a line with # for a heading like "For the sauce". Steps are separate paragraphs.
  • Import to let the helper do the typing: paste a link to a recipe page, paste copied text, or take photos of a recipe card or handwriting — more than one photo if it runs onto a second card. Check what it made, then save.
  • Equipment is for the stuff someone might not own (oven-safe skillet, stand mixer). It shows above the ingredients.
  • Name the ingredients and the times inside each step ("simmer the sauce 20 minutes") — cook mode reads them.
  • Add a variation on someone's recipe makes your own take, linked to the original. Say what you changed in the notes.

Some big recipe sites block link import. If a link fails, copy the recipe text off the page and paste it instead.

Family recipes, awards, and the original card

  • Family recipe from: if it's Mom's, Nonna's, Uncle Sal's — put their name here. The recipe gets a gold Family ribbon.
  • Award: won a cook-off? Say what ("Soup comp 2026, 1st place") and the card gets a small gold trophy.
  • The original card: photos you import from are kept with the recipe, as they are — the handwriting stays. Tap one to see it full size. You can add card photos to any recipe from Edit, up to six.

While you cook

  • Serves: type the number of people and every amount rescales. US / Metric switch is right next to it.
  • Tap an ingredient to check it off. Tap ? on one for substitutes.
  • Cook mode: one step at a time in big text, with the ingredients that step needs. Any step with a time gets a ⏱ button; tap it and a timer counts down at the bottom of the screen and beeps when it's done. The screen stays awake. Swipe or tap to move between steps.
  • + Shopping list on a recipe adds its ingredients. The Shopping list button on the front page merges every recipe you picked into one list; tick things off at the store, or Copy it into a text.
  • Print gives you one clean page — just the recipe, nothing else. On a phone, Print → Share → Save to Files makes a PDF.

Made it? Say so

  • At the bottom of every recipe: tap a star to rate it 1 to 5, leave a note on how it went, add a photo of your plate.
  • Ratings show on the cards and feed the Top rated sort.
  • ⚔ Battle pits two recipes against each other (two carbonaras, or the original vs. a variation). Only people who've made both get to vote. Standings are under ⚔ Battles on the front page.

Sharing and editing

  • Copy link (or Share on a phone) on a recipe gives you a link straight to it.
  • Everyone sees new recipes, ratings, and notes the moment they're added. No refresh needed.
  • Anyone can edit any recipe, so fix typos freely — but if you're changing how it's made, make it a variation instead.

Trash and backups

  • Delete doesn't destroy anything. The recipe goes to the Trash (link at the bottom of the front page) where anyone can put it back for 30 days. After that it's gone for good.
  • Download a backup (same spot) builds a zip of everything: every recipe including the trash, every photo and original card, the notes and ratings, plus a readable copy you can open in any browser. Do it now and then — before the holidays, say — and keep it somewhere safe.

Using it from your own AI

  • If you use Claude or another assistant that supports connectors, the box can be plugged in: "add this recipe to Mangia Mangia", "what's in the box that uses sausage?", "mark the wedding soup as Mom's", "rate the tortellini soup five stars" — all from your own chat, credited to you.
  • Ask Tony for your access code; it's a link you paste into your assistant's connector settings. No code needed to use the website itself.
